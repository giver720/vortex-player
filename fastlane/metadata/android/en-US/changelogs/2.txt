Fixed the bug that made playback stop on its own.

• Music and audio-only mode no longer stop after two minutes. The media session was never registered with the service, so Android never treated it as foreground and killed it for being idle.

A much more complete library:
• Navigable folder tree with collapsible branches, cumulative counts and breadcrumbs. Chains of empty folders are merged into a single row.
• Unified search across file name, folder and path, with grouped results and the matching fragment highlighted.
• Sort by date, name, duration, size or resolution, and switch between grid and compact list. Remembered across sessions.
• Multi-select on long press: play, favourite, add to playlist, share or delete in bulk.
• User playlists and a Favourites tab.

Other improvements:
• In-app updates: checks the repository releases, downloads the APK for your architecture and installs it.
• VLC-style aspect ratio picker with forced ratios and pinch zoom.
• Fixed stretched video and the black frame when opening the player.
