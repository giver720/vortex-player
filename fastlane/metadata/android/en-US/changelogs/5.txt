Fixed black video thumbnails.

• The frame was taken at second zero, and a great many real videos open with a fade-in or a dark intro. It now takes a frame further in (20% of the duration, with a 3 s floor and a 1 minute ceiling).
• The cache key includes that timestamp, so updating regenerates thumbnails instead of keeping the black ones already stored.
• If a thumbnail cannot be extracted, a film icon appears rather than a black rectangle indistinguishable from a failure.

Download queue actions:
• Empty the whole queue, with confirmation.
• Cancel only pending downloads, keeping the history.
• Retry every failed download at once.
• Clear finished ones.
