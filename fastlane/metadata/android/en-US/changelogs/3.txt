Downloads from Spotify links.

• Paste a track, an album or a playlist. Only metadata is read from Spotify — title, artist, album, duration and cover art; its audio is encrypted and never touched. Each song is then searched on YouTube Music.
• A ±15 s duration filter against the original track, which rules out live versions, sped-up edits and hour-long loops.
• Playlists are read in blocks of 100 and queued as they arrive, so the first song starts downloading while the rest are still being read.
• Every song is its own queue entry: retry just the one that failed without losing the rest.
• When finished, tags are rewritten (title, artist, album, track number) and the album cover is embedded, instead of keeping the YouTube video title.
• Albums and playlists are saved in their own folder, with numbered tracks.

If Spotify throttles a long playlist, you are told it came out incomplete rather than led to believe it downloaded in full.
