The limiter can now be switched off, and there are six new music presets.

• Switchable limiter. It used to be announced without being touchable. It flattens peaks, and on well-recorded material that takes the attack off cymbals and percussion: anyone who wants transients intact can now have them. It stays on by default, so leaving it alone changes nothing.

• This ties into the next item: a bass-heavy preset with the limiter on reads as "compressed", not as "more bass". Being able to switch it off is exactly what makes strong curves actually noticeable.

• Six music presets: Rock, Pop, Electronic, Urban, Acoustic and Classical. They are deliberately more pronounced than the content ones, because a preset you can't tell apart from flat is useless. Acoustic and Classical are the deliberate exception: exaggerating them would betray material that lives on its dynamic range.

• The preset list is reordered: music first, then tone shapes (bass, treble, smile, vocal, night), then content (cinema, TV, gaming, podcast). They were buried at the end of a row of fifteen. Whichever preset you had selected stays selected.

And a serious download bug:
• When downloading a playlist, files could never reach the destination folder while the download was still marked as finished. The publisher counted the files it meant to copy, not the ones it copied, so any write failure was swallowed silently: a "done" download and an empty folder, with no error to investigate. Written files are now counted for real, and if none are written the download fails with a message telling you what to do.
• If the playlist folder couldn't be created, the file ended up loose in the destination root instead of being skipped.
• A single video downloaded with "full playlist" enabled arrived named "0 - Title".
