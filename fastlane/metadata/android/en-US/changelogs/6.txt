SponsorBlock, and a library that notices changes on its own.

SponsorBlock in downloads:
• Three modes: off, mark segments as chapters so you can skip them by hand, or cut them out of the file.
• Eight selectable categories: sponsor, self-promo, subscribe reminder, intro, outro, preview, filler and non-music.
• When removing from video, cuts are forced at the exact frame so two seconds of the sponsor don't slip through.
• Spotify downloads additionally drop everything that isn't the song, so the MP3 doesn't carry the intro chatter or the sign-off.
• The policy is stored with each download: retrying repeats what was asked for then, not whatever is configured now.

Library:
• Notices content changes by itself: downloads, new files from other apps, deletions from a file manager. Debounced, so a fifty-song download triggers one rescan instead of fifty.
• Pull down to rescan manually.
• A menu switch to turn automatic detection off.

• yt-dlp updates itself once a day before processing the queue.
